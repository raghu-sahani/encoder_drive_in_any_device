Wiring & quick start

Encoder + two push buttons

ComponentESP32 connection
Encoder GND / +GND / 3.3V
Encoder CLK / DT / SWGPIO 32 / 33 / 25
Accelerator push buttonGPIO 26 ↔ button ↔ GND
Brake push buttonGPIO 27 ↔ button ↔ GND

Two physical push buttons are included in the ESP32 sketch. Use normally-open momentary buttons. Internal pull-ups are enabled. No external resistors required.

Start driving

  1. Upload this Arduino IDE sketch to the ESP32.
  2. Join ROBO-Drive-ESP32 Wi-Fi and open http://192.168.4.1.
  3. On the phone, Wi-Fi controls connect automatically.
  4. Press the encoder switch to center, hold the accelerator, and steer.

Braking wins if both buttons are pressed. After a crash, pause or lost connection, release both buttons before driving again.
